Privacy policy · Regency Tools

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who uses it

Regency Tools is an internal tool used only by Regency's own team, signed in with Regency's own Google accounts. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the account holder's permission, it reads and updates Regency's own files in Google Drive and Google Sheets, updates Regency's own Apps Script projects, and reads (never sends) business emails in Regency's own Gmail.

How the data is used

Only to run Regency's own business records and reports. The data stays in Regency's Google account and on Regency's own computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or shown to anyone outside Regency, and it is not used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
